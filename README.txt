Resurrect other main characters as followers. Feed them, keep them alive, and they will help you survive the world. 

If you are playing a certain character, that character will not be available for you to resurrect.

Characters available to resurrect are: Wilson, Willow, Wolfgang, Wendy, WX-78, Wickerbottom, Woodie, and Wes.

Fully compatible with Reign of Giants. Not fully tested with Shipwrecked yet.

-Followers will leave skeletal remains upon death. 
-Smash their skeletans to retrieve their skulls.
-Resurrect characters by putting their skulls on a resurrection stone.
-All characters have the same unique stats and abilities based on original game.
-All followers will suffer from hunger, health and sanity like the player.

Followers will:
-Follow you on command, or wander in a given location.
-Equip any weapon, armor, or hat given to them.
-Drop all items and equipment upon command. (Alt + RMB)
-Switch equipped items on command. (Ctrl + RMB)
-If they have high sanity, will raise yours when close.
-If low sanity, will drain yours when close.
-Chop fully grown/burnt trees when equipped with an Axe.
-Mine all rocks when equipped with a Pickaxe
-Search and pick up any stackable item when given that item.
-Search and forage any item, if given that item.
-Dig up any item, when given that item.
-Dig up any grave they see.
-Drop fully stacked items in their inventory.
-Catch bugs when equipped with a Net.
-Fish when equipped with a Rod.
-Attack anyone targeting you or themselves or anyone you target if equipped with a weapon.
-Flee enemies when being targetted and health is low. (Below 25% health)
-Flee enemies when being targetted and not equipped with a weapon.
-Find and eat food when hungry. (Below 25% hunger)
-Eat cooked or prepared foods when given.
-Equip or look for a light source when dark.
-Look for shelter when it rains or gets too hot.
-Look for heat source when freezing.
-Look for cold source when overheating.
-Repair and upgrade walls if they have the proper resources.
-Dry and harvest meat if they have raw meat.
-Plant and harvest crops when they have seeds.
-Bait bird traps if they have seeds.
-Retrieve and kill trapped animals.
-Examines nearby objects.
-Light campfires at night if they have fuel.
-Sleep when given bed rolls.
-Store perishables in an icebox.

Unique character abilities:
Wilson:
-Grows a beard.
-Shave him with a razor.

Willow:
-Starts fires when sanity is low.

Wolfgang:
-Feed him to grow stronger.

Wendy:
-Drops Abigails flower when in combat and ready to bloom to summon her.
-Abigail will follow and protect only Wendy.

WX-78:
-Upgrades when given gears.

Wickerbottom:
-Cannot sleep.
-Can create books when given proper recipe.
-Will read books when given.

Woody:
-Chops faster than other characters.
-Transforms into werebeaver when too many trees are chopped or is a full moon.
-As werebeaver:
-Chops/mines/digs everything he sees.
-Find and eat wood when beaverness is low.

Wes:
-Randomly makes balloons.
